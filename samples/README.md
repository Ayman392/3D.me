# samples/

One folder per part. Each has:
- measurements.txt : dimensions in mm (edit with your real caliper values)
- photos/          : drop images here (front, side, top, plain background)

Photos from online listings are for private development only.
Credit them in the report and do not redistribute.
Dimensions marked "datasheet" or "typical" are not measurements of a real part:
replace them with calipers on a real part before trusting a fit.
